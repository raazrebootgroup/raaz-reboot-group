Frequently Asked Questions

A few helpful answers before you contact our team. Requirements and availability can vary by service and case.

What services does RAAZ REBOOT GROUP provide?

Our service divisions include real estate, cleaning and hospitality, technology, company formation and business support, online services, eligible government-related assistance, and tourist visa application support for multiple destinations, subject to current eligibility and availability.

How can I ask about tourist visa assistance?

Contact us with your destination, nationality, current country of residence, and intended travel dates. We will explain the support available for your case. Visa approval is decided by the relevant authorities and cannot be guaranteed.

What documents are needed for company formation?

Requirements depend on the business activity, legal structure, ownership and approvals required. Share your proposed activity and ownership details so our team can outline the likely next steps and documents to confirm with the relevant authorities.

How much do your services cost?

Fees vary by service, scope, third-party charges and official fees where applicable. Contact us with your requirements for a case-specific quotation before proceeding.

How do I contact the team?

Use the website contact form to prepare an enquiry for WhatsApp, or message our business WhatsApp directly at +974 7187 6497.

Do you guarantee government or visa approval?

No. We can provide application guidance and administrative support where available, but decisions, processing times and official fees are controlled by the relevant authorities.
