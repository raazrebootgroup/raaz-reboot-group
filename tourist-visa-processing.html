Frequently Asked Questions

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No. Approval is solely at the discretion of the relevant embassy, immigration authority or government department.

What documents do I need?

Requirements vary by destination, nationality, residence status and personal circumstances. Contact us with those details so we can discuss the relevant checklist.

How long will processing take?

Processing times are set by the relevant authorities and can change. We cannot promise a decision date.

How much does assistance cost?

Costs depend on the destination and the scope of assistance. Ask for a case-specific quotation and confirmation of any official fees before proceeding.

Important Notice

RAAZ REBOOT GROUP provides application assistance where available and is not an embassy or government authority. Visa eligibility, decisions, official fees and processing times are determined by the relevant authorities. We do not guarantee approval. Do not send original identity documents or sensitive personal information until the requirements and secure handling process have been confirmed.
