Frequently Asked Questions

What information should I provide first?

Start with the proposed business activity, ownership plan, intended location and whether you already have a company or registration. Avoid sending sensitive documents until the requirements and handling process are clear.

How much does company formation cost?

Costs depend on the activity, legal structure, approvals and any official or third-party fees. Request a written, case-specific breakdown before proceeding.

How long does setup take?

Timelines depend on the proposed activity, document readiness and the authorities involved. We do not promise a fixed approval or completion date.

Can you guarantee a licence or approval?

No. Final approvals, eligibility decisions, official fees and processing times are determined by the relevant authorities.
